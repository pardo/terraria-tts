Reads chat messages out loud using Piper, running locally on your CPU.

- Every player gets a consistent voice, derived from a hash of their username.
- English and Spanish messages are detected automatically.
- Client-side only. Other players do not need the mod.

First launch downloads about 190 MB of speech data (the Piper engine and the
voice models) from the mod's GitHub release, because tModLoader cannot load
native code from inside a .tmod. It lands in
Documents\My Games\Terraria\tModLoader\ChatVoice\ and only happens once.
After that everything runs offline - no text ever leaves your machine.

GPL-3.0. Source: https://github.com/pardo/terraria-tts
